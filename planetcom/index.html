An AI FAQ assistant, prototyped for PlanetCom.

This is a working demo built by Rey San Madamba, Software Developer at PlanetCom, showing what an AI chat assistant could look like on planetcom.ca and planetcomcreative.ca — answering questions about Managed IT and Creative services, pulled straight from the real sites. Open the chat in the bottom corner to try it.

about this demo

What it knows.

The assistant is scoped to PlanetCom's real company info — services, history, certifications, and contact details for both the Managed IT side and PlanetCom Creative. It won't guess at pricing or make up details it wasn't given; it routes those straight to PlanetCom's real helpdesk instead.

This is a limited prototype right now, built on what's publicly available on the two sites. Feed it real internal info like pricing tiers, typical turnaround times for a job, or service plan details, and it can answer those questions directly instead of pointing people to the helpdesk every time.
