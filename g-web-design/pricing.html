FAQ

Good questions.

What's the difference between Self-Serve and Done-For-You?

With Self-Serve you get your own login and change menu items, prices, hours and photos yourself, any time. With Done-For-You you don't touch anything: send a text or email with what you want changed and we do it, as often as you like.

Do I own my website?

Yes. With Launch and Self-Serve the site is yours outright. With Done-For-You it's yours too; if you leave after the minimum term, we hand over the files.

How long does a website take?

Most small-business sites launch in 2 to 3 weeks, depending on how quickly we get your content and photos.

Can I get an email like contact@mybusiness.ca?

Yes. It's an optional $79 setup with any plan, included free with Done-For-You. You pay the email provider directly for the mailbox, usually a few dollars a month per address.

Can I upgrade later?

Anytime. Start with Launch and move to Self-Serve or Done-For-You when you're ready; you only pay the difference for the editor setup.

What about custom software, AI chat or apps?

Every one is different, so they're quoted per project after a free call about the problem you want solved or automated.
