The Elio's Feast

A boodle fight for four to six. Lechon kawali, chicken inasal, pancit, lumpia, grilled vegetables and garlic rice laid out on banana leaves. Kindly book a day ahead.

House favourite

Lechon kawali, done properly.

Pork belly fried until the skin crackles, juicy all the way through. Served with garlic rice, atchara and a little dish of spiced vinegar.

  • Dine-in or takeout
  • Lechon sauce or sukang maanghang
  • Best with an iced calamansi
