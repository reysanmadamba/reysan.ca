For Airbnb hosts

Checkout at 11. Check-in at 4.

Here is how a typical turnover day runs, so your next guests walk into a place that looks like the listing photos.

  1. 11:00Guests check out. We arrive and note any damage.
  2. 11:15Beds stripped, laundry started, dishes on.
  3. 12:00Kitchen and bathrooms scrubbed and sanitized.
  4. 1:30Dusting, floors, patio and hot tub area tidied.
  5. 2:45Beds made, towels out, supplies restocked.
  6. 3:30Photo walkthrough sent to you. Door locked.

The owners and the team

Hi, we're Raiza and Earl.

We own Metikuloso Cleaning Co., and we are a team. We work alongside our own crew of cleaners, and everyone who comes to your door follows the same checklist and the same standard we do.

Most of our work is regular house cleaning for families in Sylvan Lake and Red Deer, and turnovers for Airbnb hosts around the lake. Summers here get busy, so we know what a same-day checkout and check-in feels like.

We clean the way we would want our own place cleaned. If we spot something that needs fixing, like a dripping tap or a burnt-out bulb, we tell you before a guest does.
