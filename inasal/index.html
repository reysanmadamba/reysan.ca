Why inasal?

Real Filipino flavour, in Canada.

Inasal is the Bacolod way of grilling chicken: a tangy marinade, an annatto baste and a flame hot enough to char the edges. Craving that in Canada? That's what we specialize in on Jasper Ave, alongside pork BBQ skewers, grilled liempo, beef pares and lomi.
