Our story

A chef, a baker & a lot of brioche.

Corpuz is a family-owned donut shop in Edmonton, run by Jayson & Manilyn Corpuz.

Familiar, comforting, a little extraordinary.

We're a family-owned donut shop in Edmonton, run by Jayson and Manilyn Corpuz. Jayson brings years as a head chef; Manilyn has been a baker by passion and by profession here in Canada.

Everything is made from scratch each day. Every filling is prepared in-house from natural ingredients, with no preservatives, and folded into soft, hand-crafted brioche.

Our flavours come from everywhere we love: Japan, Thailand, Belgium and home in the Philippines, inspired by how wonderfully diverse Edmonton is.

Our customers started calling them “Extraordinary Donuts.” We took that as a promise to keep.
