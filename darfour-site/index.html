Why we exist

Two decades of war left Darfur without the basics.

Darfour Community League formed to help re-instate the essential services and needs of Darfur State after more than twenty years of destructive civil war wiped out its basic infrastructure. We're a diaspora community turning distance into action, one project at a time.
