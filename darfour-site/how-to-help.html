How to help

Three ways our community gives.

However you're able to contribute, it goes directly toward the five focus areas on our mission page — food security, water, health care, education, and power.

Community donations

Members of the Edmonton Darfur community contribute directly, in any amount, on an ongoing basis.

  • One-time or recurring gifts
  • Collected at community events
  • 100% directed to active programs

Charity partnerships

We work with established charity organizations to co-fund larger infrastructure projects.

  • Shared logistics on the ground
  • Pooled funding for bigger projects
  • Ongoing progress reporting

Business & individual support

We reach out directly to local businesses and individuals willing to sponsor a specific program.

  • Sponsor a focus area directly
  • One-off or recurring commitments
  • Recognition for major supporters
Ready to give?

Get in touch and we'll set it up.

We don't process payments through this page yet — reach out through our contact page and we'll walk you through the best way to give, whether that's an e-transfer, a cheque, or setting up a recurring contribution.
