Shadows are estimated from OpenStreetMap building data. Building heights are often missing or approximate in residential areas, so treat shadow shapes as a general guide rather than an exact measurement. Drag the orange sun icon around the property to move through the day — the time slider and shadows update to match automatically. The compass in the corner shows true north (this map doesn't rotate). Snow effect is a seasonal visual only — it isn't tied to any real snowfall data or forecast.
