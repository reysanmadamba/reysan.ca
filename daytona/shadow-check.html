Shadows are estimated from OpenStreetMap building data. Building heights are often missing or approximate in residential areas, so treat shadow shapes as a general guide rather than an exact measurement. The compass shows true north (this map doesn't rotate), and the orange dot tracks the sun's actual direction and height in the sky as you move the time slider. Snow effect is a seasonal visual only — it isn't tied to any real snowfall data or forecast.
