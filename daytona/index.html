What this demo shows

Ask it about the building process, or describe your dream home.

The assistant knows Daytona's real building process, community list, and warranty contact — pulled from daytonahomes.ca — plus a sample set of current Edmonton-area listings. Ask something like "3 bedroom under $550k near a school" and it'll recommend matching homes from that sample.

Proximity flags (near a school, grocery store, park) are illustrative placeholders in this demo — a production version would pull real geodata instead.
